01 · INITIATIVE

Unshrunk

A personal development initiative started by me around honest conversations, self-discovery and growth.

Unshrunk is my women’s empowerment initiative born from the belief that women should not have to make themselves smaller to fit into spaces, expectations, or identities that limit who they can become. Through Unshrunk, I create spaces for honest conversations, storytelling, reflection, and connection where women can explore their identity, recognise their worth, and become more confident in expressing themselves and making choices about their lives. The initiative is about liberation, freedom, voice, and the courage to take up space without apology. I hope to build a community where women feel seen, heard, supported, and encouraged to pursue their dreams, challenge limiting narratives, and live fully and unapologetically.

SHOFCO — Sustainable Livelihoods

Community-focused work supporting people to strengthen livelihoods, learning and opportunities.

As a Sustainable Livelihoods Coordinator at SHOFCO, I support community members to strengthen their economic resilience and improve their livelihoods. I coordinate livelihood initiatives, engage communities, support access to opportunities, and facilitate activities that help individuals and households build sustainable sources of income and greater self-reliance.

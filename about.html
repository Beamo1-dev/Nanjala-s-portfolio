WHO I AM

A communicator who believes every story matters.

I am Beatrice Amoit, a Journalism and Mass Communication graduate with a focus on Public Relations and Advertising. I work across journalism, corporate communication, public relations, graphic design and digital communication.

My experience includes corporate communication and customer care exposure, branding and marketing support, journalism, youth engagement, volunteering and creative design.

I have also been involved in youth and community initiatives through volunteering, scouting, environmental activities, road-safety initiatives and communication-focused programs. My approach is simple: understand the audience, find the story, communicate with intention and create something useful.
